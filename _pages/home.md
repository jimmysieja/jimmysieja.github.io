I am an Economic Research Analyst at the [Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta](https://www.atlantafed.org/), where I work with Paul Mohnen and Roberto Robatto. I earned my BBA in Business Economics from Mississippi State University in 2025, with a minor in Data Science, and was an [AEA Summer Program](https://www.aeaweb.org/about-aea/committees/aeasp) Scholar in 2024. You can view my [CV (PDF)](/files/cv.pdf).

My interests are in labor, education, and urban economics, with a focus on geographic mobility. My current work, joint with Joshua Phillips, asks whether the rise of remote work has slowed domestic "brain drain" — the movement of skilled workers away from their home states. More detail is on the [research](/research/) page.
